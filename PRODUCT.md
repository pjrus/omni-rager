# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vite, TypeScript, Three.js and GSAP, chosen for a lightweight single-page WebGL experience with strong animation control.

## Users

Creative-technology clients, collaborators and hiring teams exploring a developer's work on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present a portfolio as a tactile, cinematic device: visitors browse and open project case studies by physically manipulating a Three.js selector instead of using a conventional navigation bar.

## Positioning

The navigation, project state and visual identity are one interactive mechanical object; project transitions are expressed through its movement, light and synchronised sound.

## Operating Context

A single-session exploratory experience, typically entered from a shared portfolio link. Visitors may use mouse, keyboard, wheel or touch, and may be in an environment where sound is inappropriate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five placeholder projects with title, description, category, technology tags, preview visual and external action.
- Drag, wheel, keyboard and direct-button alternatives for browsing; central control selects; back resets the active state.
- Three.js procedural geometry with independently animated mechanical groups, responsive rendering and a reduced-motion path.
- Locally stored Omnitrix-inspired sound samples sourced from the user-provided MyInstants search page, subject to the source's availability and usage terms. The time-out sample is intentionally excluded.
- Flat colours only: no CSS gradients or visual-effect overlays.
- Project names, descriptions and links are illustrative and must be replaced before public launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Working name: Omni Rager.
- Dark, cinematic, futuristic and tactile; the device remains the visual hero.
- The Omnitrix is the conceptual reference for behaviour, sound and mechanical interaction, without using protected logos or character artwork.
- Minimal, modern typography and non-traditional navigation.

## Evidence on Hand

No real project case studies, personal biography, client proof, photography, brand assets or production links were supplied. Future work must not present placeholders as real claims.

## Product Principles

- Make interaction feel physical before making it decorative.
- Keep every sound tied to a visible mechanical event.
- Let project information orbit the device without competing with it.
- Preserve responsive, keyboard-accessible alternatives to gestures.
- Scale rendering complexity to device capability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, mute control, reduced motion, high-contrast text and non-gesture alternatives for every essential action.
